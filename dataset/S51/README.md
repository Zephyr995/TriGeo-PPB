To get S51 dataset, you must first download PDBbind_v2021.

Following https://github.com/TEKHOO/PPAP, you can get PDBbind_v2021 PDB files as below:

Visit the official download page: https://www.pdbbind-plus.org.cn/download.
Register for a free account on the website if you do not already have one.
Log in to your account.
Navigate to the Other Datasets section.
Locate and download the PPAP Dataset.
